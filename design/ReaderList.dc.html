THE CRAFT OF TYPE
THE PAGE

A page is not a container that text is poured into. It is a grid of line boxes.

 
–  A block may begin on any row, but not between two.
–  Space is measured in rows.
–  A row holds one line.
 

The third of those is the one that surprises people, and it is the one the reader cannot bend.
