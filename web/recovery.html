· XTEINK X3 & X4

BOOT THE OTHER SLOT

Encre is always written to slot 2, so slot 1 still holds the firmware the reader came with. This points the reader back at it. It takes a few seconds and erases nothing.

SAVE A COPY OF THE READER

Reads all 16 MB and saves it to this computer. This takes about 25 minutes.

WRITE A SAVED COPY BACK

Writes a 16 MB copy you saved earlier. It replaces everything on the reader, Encre included.

A saved copy belongs to the reader it came from. Do not write one reader's copy to another.
