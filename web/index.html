· XTEINK X3 & X4

PUT ENCRE ON YOUR READER

  1. Plug the reader into this computer. Some cables only carry power. Those will not work.
  2. Hold the power button until the screen changes. A sleeping reader does not show up in the list of devices.

Encre runs on the X3 and the X4 from one build. Screen rotation has only been checked on the X3.

Encre always goes into the reader's second firmware slot, so the firmware it came with is never written over. Some readers are laid out differently. On those, installing replaces everything, and the page saves a full copy of the reader first. That read takes about 25 minutes.
